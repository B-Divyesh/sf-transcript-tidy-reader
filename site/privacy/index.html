Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 30 August 2026

What the extension reads

The extension reads the current caption track or visible transcript only when you select “Tidy this transcript.”

It does not read captions in the background.

Where transcript text goes

Parsing, paragraph reflow, search, and export happen on your device.

Browser extension storage holds the active transcript. A previous active Plus state can also hold a local shelf.

We do not receive or host transcript text.

Licenses

Transcript Tidy Plus is a $12 one-time local shelf for up to 50 recent reads.

Billing registration is pending. The site and extension do not make license-check requests until that dependency is available.

Analytics and outside code

The site and extension have no advertising, behavioural analytics, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts.

The extension sends no page-view events.

Your controls

Remove transcript data and a local Plus shelf by clearing extension data or uninstalling it.

Contact

For privacy requests, email privacy@sociobot.in.
