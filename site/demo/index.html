The quiet power of a useful pause

In a difficult conversation, silence can feel like an empty space we need to fill. A useful pause does something different. It gives each person enough room to hear what was actually said.

The pause is not an escape from the discussion. It is a small piece of structure. You can breathe, check your first reaction, and choose the next sentence with care.

Try counting one quiet breath before you reply today. The goal is not perfect calm. The goal is to make the next useful thought easier to find.
